# Code of Conduct

RepoLens MCP contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, specific, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus critique on code, tests, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Explain security or reliability concerns with enough detail for another maintainer to reproduce or evaluate them.
- Respect private repository data. Do not request, publish, or infer secrets, proprietary source, or confidential business logic from generated graph artifacts.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private security reports before coordinated disclosure.
- Sharing private source metadata, generated databases, or graph packages without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to blocked participation in the project.

Security issues should be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
